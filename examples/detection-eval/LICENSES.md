# Inputs and their licenses

## Photos
CC0 or public domain only, 1024 px renditions from Flickr, found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and checked
on each photo's page (2026-10-13). `qa/fetch_inputs.py` downloads them from a public mirror, or else from Flickr, and
checks them against `inputs.lock.yaml`. They are used as they are, never re-encoded.

- **CC0 1.0**: the author dedicated the photo to the public domain.
- **Public Domain Mark 1.0**: marked as free of known copyright by its uploader. Most are works of the US federal
  government (USDA, National Park Service, US Army accounts) or photos old enough to be in the public domain, from
  archives (Queensland State Archives, Dunedin City Council Archives, Newcastle Libraries, UNC Libraries). The others
  were marked by their authors.

No identifiable person is the subject of a photo: people are far away, from behind, masked or blurred by motion.

## Composites
`slices/small-objects/*.jpg` are built by `qa/make_small_objects.py` from the photos above: people and cars cut out of
the indoor, outdoor and warehouse photos (along silhouettes from torchvision's Mask R-CNN v2), pasted on the
backgrounds. They are CC0, like the recipe in `slices/small-objects/labels.json`.

## Labels
The `labels.json` files are ours, CC0. The photos' labels were proposed by torchvision's Faster R-CNN ResNet-50 FPN v2
(COCO weights) with `qa/propose_labels.py`, and nobody reviewed them: see SPEC.md.

## The model
`qa/models.py` downloads torchvision's SSDlite320 MobileNetV3-Large COCO weights from `download.pytorch.org`, checked
by SHA-256 (`inputs.lock.yaml`). torchvision is BSD-3-Clause. The weights are not part of this repository.
