# robotics-3d: odometry and mapping (`kitefin/slam`)

## Purpose
- **Shows:** QA-Board on robotics data: 6DoF trajectories against ground truth, point clouds, camera videos played in
  sync with the reference, error over time, and History on a trajectory metric.
- **Audience:** robotics, perception and automotive engineers.
- **Phase 3.**

## What the code computes
Python (numpy, scipy; ffmpeg for videos). Everything is simulated, so the ground truth is exact.

- **World:** a procedural warehouse per sequence: shelves, pallets, pillars and walls as boxes, from the sequence seed.
- **Robot and sensors:** a K3 drives a trajectory (splines with speed profiles). Depth camera 160x120 at 15 fps, 70°
  horizontal field of view, ray-cast against the boxes, noise σ = 1% of depth, dropouts at depth edges. IMU at 200 Hz,
  gyro and accelerometer noise with a bias random walk.
- **Odometry:** voxel downsampling, point-to-plane ICP from each frame to the last keyframe (`scipy.spatial.cKDTree`),
  started from the IMU's prediction, which stays in the cost as a prior (`imu_weight`). Keyframes' depth is smoothed
  (edge-preserving) for cleaner normals. Translation directions that few points constrain (along an aisle or a
  corridor) follow the prior: ICP can't see them, and its noise would decide. A new keyframe after `keyframe_dist`
  meters or `keyframe_angle` degrees.
- **Loop closure** (flag): candidates are the older keyframes the odometry puts nearby (by position and heading, a
  coarse occupancy signature breaks ties), verified by ICP. A match is a loop closure when `loop_min_overlap` of the
  new keyframe's points land on the old one's surfaces; then the keyframes' pose graph is solved with
  `scipy.optimize.least_squares`.
- **Evaluation:** ATE RMSE after SE(3) alignment, RPE (translation %, rotation °/m), drift at the end, loop closures,
  time per frame.
- **Memoized:** a run is keyed by the code, the flags, the sequence and the seed; a commit that doesn't change them
  reuses the identical run, time per frame included. The story's fillers cost nothing.

**Flags** (`build/flags.json`; defaults are the head of `main` at T0, the story starts from `icp_iterations: 20`,
`keyframe_dist: 0.3`, `loop_closure: false`):

| Flag | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `voxel_size` | meters | `0.05` |
| `icp_iterations` | 5-50 | `15` |
| `keyframe_dist`, `keyframe_angle` | meters, degrees | `0.6`, `10` |
| `imu_weight` | 0-1 | `0.6` |
| `loop_closure` | `true`, `false` | `true` |
| `loop_min_overlap` | 0-1 | `0.6` |

## Inputs and licenses
All generated by `qa/make_sequences.py`, ours: `sequences/<name>/` holds the world seed, the trajectory, and the
ground-truth poses and point clouds. Nothing is downloaded. Public datasets (TUM RGB-D, EuRoC, KITTI) are not used:
their licenses are CC BY or non-commercial.

| Sequence | What it stresses |
|---|---|
| `aisle-sweep` | long straight aisles, U-turns |
| `loop` | a closed loop around a block of shelves: loop closure |
| `figure8` | two loops, crossing in the middle (less than a lap in 40 s: no loop closure) |
| `figure8-fast` | the same at twice the speed, 2.4 laps: fast rotations, loop closures |
| `docking` | slow approach and rotation in front of a wall |
| `long-corridor` | few features along the direction of travel: degenerate for ICP |

600 frames (40 s) each.

## Visualizations (`qaboard.yaml`)

```yaml
outputs:
  metrics: qa/metrics.yaml
  visualizations:
    - name: Trajectory
      path: poses.tsv                    # the ground truth is GT_final.txt
      type: 6dof/txt
    - name: Depth camera
      path: camera.webm                  # depth images and the path so far, played in sync with the reference
    - name: Position error
      path: ate.plotly.json
      side_by_side: false
    - name: Map, from above
      path: map.png                      # mapped points and estimated path over the true layout
    - name: Map in 3D
      path: map.pcd
      type: pointcloud/pcd
      path_groundtruth: map.truth.pcd
      default_hidden: true
    - name: Loop closures
      path: loop_closures.json
      default_hidden: true
```

- **6DoF** (`webapp/src/viewers/slam/SlamOutputCard.jsx`): a TSV file with a header row, a `t` column in seconds and
  one column per pose value (`x y z yaw pitch roll`, degrees). The ground truth is read from `GT_final.txt` in the run's output
  directory, in the same format. The viewer now takes the card's width, with the 3D view at equal axis scales.
- **Point clouds** (`webapp/src/viewers/pointcloud/`, new for this project): PCD files (ascii or binary, `x y z`), the
  new run's, the reference's and an optional ground truth in the run's folder, on the same 3D axes. The old ToF viewer
  (`pointcloud/txt`, `Frame<N>/pointcloud.pcd` and depth maps per frame) stays for its users.
- **Video:** VP9 in WebM, like the camera ISP's: Playwright's Chromium has no H.264.

## Story (`story.yaml`)
About 20 commits, mostly by mira, some by arjun. The plot: **loop closure lands with false matches that pull the
figure-8s meters away, until its threshold goes up; then a change that trusts ICP over the IMU makes the figure-8s
drift, History and the gate show it, it's reverted; keyframes every 0.6 m make it faster and better.**

| When | Branch | Author | Change | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| -38d | `main` | mira | **`fw-26-08`**: ICP to the last keyframe, IMU prior, `keyframe_dist: 0.3`, `loop_closure: false` | milestone `fw-26.08` |
| -31d to -24d | `slam/loop-closure` | mira | **`lc-candidates`**: `loop_closure: true, loop_min_overlap: 0.3`; merged (**`merge-loop-closure`**) | false closures: `figure8`, `figure8-fast` ATE ×3 or worse; the gate fails |
| -23d | `main` | mira | **`loop-overlap`**: `loop_min_overlap: 0.6` | vs `fw-26-08`: `figure8-fast` ATE −15% or better, `loop` −8% or better |
| -20d | `main` | mira | **`fw-26-09`**: release | milestone `fw-26.09` |
| -16d | `main` | arjun | **`imu-trust-icp`**: `imu_weight: 0.2` | `figure8` ATE +40% or worse, `figure8-fast` +15%; the gate fails |
| -15d to -8d | `main` | both | fillers | the regression stays in History |
| -6d | `main` | mira | **`imu-revert`**: `imu_weight: 0.6` | `figure8` back within 5% of `fw-26-09` |
| -2d | `main` | mira | **`keyframe-dist`**: `keyframe_dist: 0.6`, and a `tune-keyframes` sweep | `figure8-fast` ATE −12% or better, time per frame −15%; the gate passes |
| -0d 15:00 | `main` | mira | **`latest`**: `icp_iterations: 15` | `ci` still running at T0 |

The figure-8 (1.15 m/s) covers less than a lap in 40 s: it never comes back to a place, and loop closure can only hurt
it. Earlier plans had `keyframe_dist: 0.6` as the regression: measured, it lowers the error everywhere.

**Checks:** the "Shows" column. **Milestones:** `fw-26.08` and `fw-26.09`, by mira.

## Determinism and budget
- Deterministic on one machine (seeded worlds and sensors); `time_per_frame` is measured.
- The whole story: ≤ 20 min on a 4-CPU runner, thanks to memoization: 5 distinct `ci` computations and a sweep of 6 combinations on 2 sequences.
