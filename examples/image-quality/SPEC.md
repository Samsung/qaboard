# image-quality: the camera ISP (`kitefin/camera-isp`)

## Purpose
- **Shows:** QA-Board's image tools on a camera pipeline: synced zoom, Image Diff and its threshold, pixel values, ROI
  histograms, auto-ROI, filters, deep zoom on a 113-megapixel image, per-stage dynamic outputs, metrics with targets,
  tuning, and bit-accuracy checks of a fixed-point model.
- **Audience:** camera/ISP and imaging engineers, embedded teams handing an algorithm to silicon. It is also the
  tutorial's template (`qa init --template image-quality`), so its code stays short and readable.
- **Phase 1:** the vertical slice is built on it.

## What the code computes
Python (numpy, scipy, Pillow, tifffile, imagecodecs). `qa/main.py` simulates the K3 camera, runs the ISP, and measures the result
against the scene it started from.

1. **Scene:** the input, converted to linear RGB.
2. **Sensor:** optics blur (Gaussian PSF), RGGB Bayer mosaic, exposure and analog gain from the configuration, shot noise
   (Poisson) and read noise (Gaussian) from a fixed noise model, black level 64, 12-bit quantization. Integer math
   only (the shot and read noise are drawn as one Gaussian of the same variance), so the raw is the same on every
   machine. Seeded per input and `$QA_SEED` (the story's seed), not per commit.
3. **ISP**, one image per stage in `stages/` (`<n>.jpg`, quality 85, on `linux`; `<n>.png` on `hw-model`, see below):
   1. black level and white balance, then the raw mosaic shown as a false-color image (`1`);
   2. demosaic: `bilinear` or `hamilton-adams` (edge-directed) (`2`);
   3. denoise: `gaussian` or `bilateral`, on luma and chroma (`3`);
   4. sharpen: unsharp mask with a detail boost, optionally clamped by local contrast (`4`);
   5. tone map (filmic curve), sRGB, 8 bits (`5`; the output is also `out.png`, lossless).
   Stages 3 and 4 work on companded values (square roots of linear light) in luma/chroma.
4. **Measures:** PSNR and SSIM against the ideal output (the scene without blur or noise, through the same tone
   curve); on charts, noise in flat patches, MTF50 and edge overshoot from the slanted edge (ISO 12233-style ESF → LSF →
   MTF), ΔE2000 on the color chart.
5. **Videos** (`qa/video.py`, inputs `clips/*.yaml`): what the camera records while the robot drives, a 640x360
   window moving across a photo. Every frame goes through the sensor (new noise each frame) and the ISP, and is piped to
   ffmpeg: `out.webm`, VP9, one thread, so the same frames give the same file. VP9 because every browser plays it,
   including the Chromium the docs are captured with (no H.264). Measures: the mean PSNR of the frames.

**Platforms**
- `linux`: the float pipeline (float32).
- `hw-model`: the same pipeline as the camera chip computes it, in integers: 12-bit input, 16-bit datapath, Q-format
  coefficients, LUTs for bilateral weights and the tone curve, a configurable rounding mode. Integer math makes its
  outputs bit-exact across machines, so `qa check-bit-accuracy` can compare commits. It also writes `raw/<stage>.u16`
  (each stage's 16-bit buffer, in a 256x256 window at the center of the frame, like a debug port: `DUMP_WINDOW` in
  `regs.txt`), `stages/<n>.png` (the same window, 8-bit), `regs.txt` (the register values the chip would be programmed
  with, LUTs included), and `fxp_psnr`: PSNR of its output against the float pipeline's.

**Flags** (`build/flags.json`; defaults are the head of `main` at T0):

| Flag | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `demosaic` | `bilinear`, `hamilton-adams` | `hamilton-adams` |
| `denoise` | `gaussian`, `bilateral` | `bilateral` |
| `denoise_strength` | 0.5-3.0 | `1.6` |
| `detail_boost` | 1.0-2.0 | `1.5` |
| `sharpen_clamp` | `none`, `local-contrast` | `local-contrast` |
| `tone_curve` | `gamma`, `filmic` | `filmic` |
| `tile_size` | pixels, `0` = the whole frame at once (the 113 MP wall; the other inputs fit in memory) | `512` |
| `fxp_rounding` | `truncate`, `half-up`, `half-even` | `half-even` |
| `fxp_bilateral_guard_bits` | 0-4 | `2` |
| `chroma_denoise_low_light` | `true`: an extra chroma pass when gain ≥ 16 | absent (`main` sets it at -0d, still in CI at T0) |

Tuning parameters override flags of the same name (`denoise_strength`, `detail_boost`).

**Configurations** (`configs` in batches): `base` (ISO 100), `low-light` (ISO 3200), `night` (ISO 12800). They set
gain and exposure, so they show as tags on runs.

## Inputs and licenses

| Input | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `charts/slanted-edge.png`, `siemens-star.png`, `color-chart.png` (24 patches), `zone-plate.png`, `flat-gray.png` | Generated by `qa/make_charts.py` at first run (1224x816 each) | Ours (Apache-2.0, like the repo) |
| `charts/test-wall-113mp.tiff` (12288 x 9216) | Generated: a mosaic of all charts at several scales, gradients, rendered text, and crops of the photos below | Ours, plus the photos' licenses |
| `photos/*.png`, 8 photos | Downloaded, checked, cropped to 3:2 and resized to 1224x816 (Pillow Lanczos) | CC0 or public domain only |
| `clips/aisle-drive.yaml` | A video made from `photos/warehouse-aisle.png`: 72 frames at 24 fps, the window panning up the aisle | The photo's |

- **Photos** cover what an ISP gets wrong: `night-street` (point lights, noise), `foliage` (fine texture),
  `facade` (straight edges: moiré, halos), `sky-gradient` (banding), `warehouse-aisle` (the robot's view, mixed light),
  `fabric-colors` (saturated colors, false color), `backlit-window` (high contrast: halos), `stone-texture` (detail vs
  noise).
- **Sources:** Wikimedia Commons files tagged CC0 or public domain, US federal works (NASA, USGS, NPS, USFWS: check
  each file's page, some are credited to third parties), or photos a maintainer took and releases under CC0. Not
  ESA/Hubble (CC BY), Unsplash or Pexels (their own licenses). No identifiable person as the subject, no prominent
  logo.
- Each photo is listed in `inputs.lock.yaml`, one entry per photo (SHA-256 and size of the resized PNG, hash of its
  pixels, license, source page, URL and SHA-256 of the rendition it was made from, crop), and in `LICENSES.md` (title,
  author, source page, license, retrieval date). Everyone, CI included, downloads them from a public mirror,
  `https://qaboard-media.sivoov.app/inputs/image-quality/<sha256>.png` (bucket `qaboard-media`, immutable, never
  overwritten), checked against the SHA-256; Wikimedia is only the fallback for a photo not mirrored yet. Inputs are
  1224x816 (about 1 MP), not 4 MP: larger inputs don't fit the `ci` budget below.
- **ROIs:** each photo has a `<name>.metadata.yaml` (ours) with 2-3 named regions (`roi:`), read by `metadata()` in
  `qa/main.py`, so the viewer's ROI menu has presets next to the auto-ROIs.
- **113 MP output:** written as a tiled (256x256), multi-resolution TIFF with JPEG tiles (quality 90), `out.tiff`
  (about 25 MB), which the viewer prefers over `out.png` and Cantaloupe can serve as a pyramid without decoding the
  whole image per tile. Nothing else is written for the wall (no stages, no `raw/`). Checked in phase 1 with the
  `cantaloupe` image (5.0.5, Java2dProcessor, GeoSolutions TIFF reader): it uses the levels stored as successive IFDs,
  and ignores SubIFDs. With successive IFDs, zoomed-out IIIF requests take 0.04-0.3 s; with SubIFDs (or one level) they
  decode the full resolution, 1-2 s each. `out.tiff` stores its levels as successive IFDs.

## Metrics (`qa/metrics.yaml`)

| Key | Label | Unit (`suffix`, `scale`) | `smaller_is_better` | `target` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `psnr` | PSNR | ` dB` | false | 30 |
| `ssim` | SSIM | | false | 0.9 |
| `noise_flat` | Noise in flat patches | ` DN` (8-bit) | true | 2.0 |
| `mtf50` | MTF50 | ` cy/px` | false | 0.25 |
| `overshoot` | Edge overshoot (halos) | `%`, scale 100 | true | 0.05 |
| `delta_e` | Color error ΔE2000 | | true | 5 |
| `fxp_psnr` | Fixed-point vs float | ` dB` | false | 45 |
| `runtime` | Runtime | ` s` | true | |
| `is_failed` | Crashed | `%`, scale 100 | true | 0 |

Chart metrics only exist on the charts they need. `default_metric: psnr`;
`summary_metrics: [psnr, ssim, overshoot, mtf50, noise_flat]`; `main_metrics: [psnr, ssim, overshoot, mtf50, runtime]`.
Gate rules (experimental): `{metric: psnr, max_regression: 1%}`, `{metric: overshoot, max_regression: 20%}`.

## Story (`story.yaml`)
About 35 commits over 6 weeks. The plot: **a denoiser that wins PSNR but adds halos**, merged because the numbers
looked good, caught in the images, fixed with a clamp.

| When | Branch | Author | Change (flags) | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| -42d to -36d | `main` | tobias, ines | pipeline basics: noise model, `demosaic: hamilton-adams`, `tone_curve: filmic` | MTF50 up, false colors down |
| -35d | `main` | ines | **`isp-4-1`**: release, `denoise: gaussian, detail_boost: 1.2` | milestone `isp-4.1` |
| -31d to -29d | `fxp/round-half-even` | tobias | **`fxp-round-half-even`**: `fxp_rounding: half-even`; merged | `check-bit-accuracy` fails (sharpen and tone-map files differ), `fxp_psnr` +0.2 dB or more |
| -24d to -21d | `isp/bilateral-denoise` | ines | **`bilateral-denoise`**: `denoise: bilateral, denoise_strength: 1.6, detail_boost: 1.5`; a local run `ines-halo`; the `tune-denoise` sweep (strength × boost, 12 combinations) | `low-light` PSNR +0.3 dB or more, noise −25% or more, **overshoot +80% or more** (about 4% → 11%) |
| -20d | `main` | ines | **`merge-bilateral`** | History: overshoot jumps |
| -14d to -10d | `isp/halo-clamp` | ines | **`halo-clamp`**: `sharpen_clamp: local-contrast`; merged | overshoot ≤ 5.5%, PSNR within 0.15 dB of `bilateral-denoise` |
| -8d | `main` | ines | **`isp-4-2`**: release | milestone `isp-4.2` |
| -6d | `main` | tobias | **`one-tile`**: `tile_size: 0` | the 113 MP run dies over its memory limit (`lsf-memlimit`) |
| -6d +3h | `main` | tobias | **`one-tile-fix`**: `tile_size: 512` | |
| -3d to -1d | `fxp/guard-bits` | tobias | **`fxp-guard-bits`**: `fxp_bilateral_guard_bits: 2` | `fxp_psnr` 43 → 47 dB or close; bit-accuracy differs, as expected |
| -0d 14:10 | `main` | ines | **`chroma-low-light`**: `chroma_denoise_low_light: true` | `ci` still running at T0 (50% done, 3 running) |

Measured (in-process replay of the checks, seed 1203): MTF50 +18% with Hamilton-Adams; `fxp_psnr` 44.5 → 47.3 dB with
half-even rounding; with the bilateral denoiser, `low-light` PSNR +1.6 dB, noise in flat patches −49%, overshoot
4.2% → 11.0%; with the clamp, overshoot 5.0% and PSNR +0.1 dB; `fxp_psnr` 43.6 → 47.2 dB with the guard bits.

- `defaults.flags` is the state before the story: `demosaic: bilinear, denoise: gaussian, denoise_strength: 1.0,
  detail_boost: 1.0, sharpen_clamp: none, tone_curve: gamma, fxp_rounding: truncate, fxp_bilateral_guard_bits: 0`.
- `deep-zoom` runs on `isp-4-1`, `merge-bilateral`, `isp-4-2`, `one-tile`, `one-tile-fix`; `nightly` on the releases;
  `video` on `isp-4-1`, `merge-bilateral` and `isp-4-2`, as its own batch (label `video`, so the CI batch's runs and
  counts don't change): the halos around the packages, then their fix.
- Every commit runs `ci`. `bitexact` runs on the root commit, the releases, the `fxp/` commits and their merges, the
  parent of `fxp-guard-bits`, and one filler (`docs-tuning-guide`, for the "same" bit-accuracy check). Fillers (docs,
  refactors, CI) keep their parent's flags, so their outputs are byte for byte their parent's.
- **Checks:** the "Shows" column, plus a hook `bit_accuracy(ctx, vs, expect, only)` that runs `qa check-bit-accuracy`
  and expects `differ` on `fxp-round-half-even` (only stage 4 and 5 files, `out.png` and `regs.txt`) and
  `fxp-guard-bits`, `same` on a filler.
- **Milestones:** `isp-4.1` on `isp-4-1` and `isp-4.2` on `isp-4-2`, by ines.

## Determinism and budget
- Fully deterministic except `runtime`: noise is seeded per input and the story seed, the integer pipeline is
  bit-exact, the float pipeline is deterministic on one machine (and close across machines). Checked: two runs of
  `ci` and `bitexact` give the same 204 output files, byte for byte. PNG bytes also depend on the zlib Pillow was built
  with; `raw/*.u16` and `regs.txt` don't.
- The whole story: ≤ 30 min on a 4-CPU runner (35 `ci`, 10 `bitexact`, 2 `nightly`, 5 `deep-zoom`, one sweep), and
  ≤ 2 GB of results (about 1.4 GB of it is `ci`; fillers' outputs are identical to their parent's, so a dedupe by
  hardlinks would save most of it).
- First run: generating the charts and the 113 MP wall takes about a minute; they are cached in the inputs folder.
