# Inputs and their licenses

## Charts
`inputs/charts/*` are generated by `qa/make_charts.py`: slanted edge, Siemens star, color chart, zone plate, flat gray,
and the 113 MP test wall. They are ours, under the repository's license (Apache-2.0). The color chart uses the
commonly published approximate sRGB values of a 24-patch color rendition chart. The wall's text uses Pillow's default
font, Aileron (public domain, CC0). The wall also contains crops of the photos below.

## Photos
CC0 or public domain only, downloaded by `qa/fetch_photos.py` from their source (`inputs.lock.yaml`). Being selected:
see `inputs.lock.yaml`.
