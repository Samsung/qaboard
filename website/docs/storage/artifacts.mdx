Artifacts are the files a commit needs to run again: its build, wrapper scripts, trained models or calibration data.
Your CI saves them with `qa save-artifacts`, in the results storage. QA-Board then uses them to redo runs and to
[tune from the web app](/docs/tuning-from-the-webapp), without a checkout or a build.

## Save them in CI {#how-to-save-artifacts}
1. List the files in *qaboard.yaml*, in named groups of glob patterns:

   ```yaml title="qaboard.yaml"
   artifacts:
     binary:
       glob: build/my-tool
     configurations:
       globs:
       - configurations/*.json
       - "*.yaml"
   ```

2. In your CI job, save them after the build, before the runs:

   ```bash
   make
   qa save-artifacts
   qa batch ci
   ```

`qa save-artifacts` always saves *qaboard.yaml*, *qa/*, the metrics and batches files, and the *.envrc* files, even
without `artifacts` in *qaboard.yaml*. Patterns are relative to the project's root.

- To save only some groups: `qa save-artifacts binary`. The files `qa` always saves are then left out.
- To save other files: `qa save-artifacts --file 'build/*'`.
