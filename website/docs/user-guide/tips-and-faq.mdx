Answers to the questions people ask in their first weeks with QA-Board, the keyboard shortcuts worth learning, and a
few tricks.

## FAQ

### Where do I start?
Follow [Your first review](/docs/user-guide/first-review): it walks through one commit of the demo projects, from the
commit list to the pixel that changed. The [overview](/docs/user-guide/overview) explains the few words you need.

### I can't find my commit
- Search for it: type a word of its message or the start of its hash in the search box of the commit list. It looks
  at all the commits, while the list only shows the last 3 days. See
  [Find your commit](/docs/user-guide/finding-results#find-your-commit).
- Only commits **with results** are listed. If your CI didn't run QA-Board on it yet, the commit doesn't exist for
  QA-Board.
- Check you're in the right **project**: in a monorepo, each sub-project is a separate project.

### Was my commit better or worse?
Open the commit page: the **Summary** compares the median of each metric with the reference's, in green when it got
better and in red when it got worse. Then the **Metrics Diff** shows each test's change. See
[Reading metrics](/docs/user-guide/metrics).

### What is my commit compared with?
By default, with the latest commit with results on the project's reference branch, when you opened the page. It shows
in the second bar, the reference's. If it's not what you expected, change it as explained in
[choose another reference](/docs/user-guide/comparing-results#picking-a-reference), and check the reference's **batch**
and **filter** too.

### Where do the two images differ?
In an image viewer, pick the difference mode (or press <kbd>4</kbd>), or open the regions of interest menu to let
QA-Board find where they differ most. See
[Find where images differ](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#find-where-images-differ).

### Why did my run fail?
Open the commit page's **Logs** view: a failed run's log opens on its last lines, where the error usually is. See
[Why did my run fail?](/docs/user-guide/logs#why-did-my-run-fail).

### What does an orange ≠ tag on a run mean? {#reference-mismatch-tags}
Its reference run is not exactly the same test: the closest match used another configuration (**Config**), input
(**Input**) or platform (**Platform**). Hover the tag to see how they differ. Make the reference's filter match the new
one, or pick a reference batch with the same tests. See
[How runs are matched](/docs/user-guide/comparing-results#how-runs-are-matched).

### The page says "No results" or "Error"
- **No results**: the commit's CI batch has no finished runs. Check the CI pipeline, or pick another batch in the
  batch selector: the commit may only have tuning or local runs.
- An **Error** tag next to the commit: hover it. A `404` usually means a wrong commit hash, or runs saved in another
  project. A `500` is a server error: contact your QA-Board admins.

### A run is stuck as pending
If it's really dead (e.g. killed by the cluster), use **Mark as failed** in the run's ☰ menu, then **Redo** it. See
[Redo, stop and clean up runs](/docs/user-guide/running-from-the-webapp#redo-stop-and-clean-up-runs).

### Did my batch start? Is the cluster busy?
Open **Runs activity**, from your account menu or the project's sidebar: what waits and runs now, what ended
recently, and who loads the queues. See [Runs activity](/docs/user-guide/runs-activity).

### I'm over my storage quota
Open **Storage usage**, from your account menu or the project's sidebar: your biggest batches, commits and folders,
and how to delete their files. See [Storage usage](/docs/user-guide/storage-usage).

### I can't see a project, or the buttons are grayed out
Sign in first: redoing runs, tuning and saving milestones need it. If you're signed in, you may need another role on
the project: its **Access** tab says yours, and **Request access** asks its owners. See
[Project access](/docs/user-guide/project-access).

### How do I switch to the dark theme?
Click the theme button next to **What's new**, at the top of the sidebar: it goes from your system's theme to light,
then dark. Or pick it in **Settings → Preferences**. Plots, editors and viewers follow the theme.

### My local runs don't show up
Runs started from your workspace only appear in QA-Board with `qa --share`, see
[QA CLI tips](/docs/using-the-qa-cli). They show up in a batch named after you (`you 🏠 label`), with a warning that
they may not match the committed code.

### How do I compare with the last release?
On the commit page, open the **Change** menu of the reference's bar and pick the release's milestone. See
[Milestones](/docs/user-guide/milestones).

### How do I show a colleague what I see?
Copy the page's link from your browser: it holds the commits, batches, filters, the view and how images are compared,
but not the zoom. See [Sharing](/docs/user-guide/sharing).

### Where are the files?
On a shared storage. Open a run's folder with the folder icon of its card, copy paths from the ☰ menus, or see
[Where is the data saved?](/docs/storage/where-is-the-data).

### Why does the "What's new" dialog show up again?
"Seen" release notes are remembered by your browser. A new browser, another computer or a private window doesn't
know you've seen them. See [What's new](/docs/user-guide/whats-new).

### I'd like a new feature, or I found a bug
Tell your QA-Board admins, with the page's link. See
[Report a bug or ask for help](/docs/user-guide/sharing#bug-reports-and-support).

## Keyboard shortcuts
Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere in the web app (or click the **?** button in the sidebar) for a cheat sheet of the
shortcuts, QA-Board's words and the filter syntax. The most useful shortcuts:

| Key | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>?</kbd> | Everywhere | Open or close the cheat sheet. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> / <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>, or <kbd>/</kbd> | Everywhere | [Search](/docs/user-guide/finding-results#command-palette) for a project, a branch, a commit, a batch or a page, and go there. |
| <kbd>o</kbd> | Commit page | Show or hide the [view options](/docs/user-guide/commit-page#view-options). |
| <kbd>1</kbd>-<kbd>4</kbd> | Image and video viewers | Side by side, wipe, single image, difference. |
| <kbd>t</kbd> | Image, video and text viewers | Swap new and reference in place, to flip between them. |
| <kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>-</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd> | Image and video viewers | Zoom in, zoom out, fit. |
| <kbd>f</kbd> | Image and video viewers | Full screen. |
| <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Videos | Play or pause, previous or next frame. |
| <kbd>r</kbd>, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>r</kbd> | Commit page, with several references | Compare with the next or previous reference. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> / <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>{'\\'}</kbd> | Everywhere | Collapse or expand the sidebar. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Dialogs, menus, What's new | Close. |

Image viewers' keys work anywhere on the page: they go to the viewer under your mouse, else the one you used last,
else the first one in view. Video viewers' keys go to the one you clicked, else the one under your mouse. Shortcuts are
ignored while you type in a text box.

Every viewer and tool has more:
- **Images**: pixel values, blind tests, regions of interest... See
  [the image viewer's shortcuts](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#keyboard-shortcuts), or click the keyboard button of a
  viewer.
- **Videos**: speed, first and last frame, where the videos differ most... See
  [the video viewer's shortcuts](/docs/user-guide/video-viewer#keyboard-shortcuts).
- **The file browser**: filter, go to a path, copy paths... See
  [its shortcuts](/docs/user-guide/files#keyboard-shortcuts), or press <kbd>?</kbd> in it.

| Key | Where | What it does |

## Tricks worth knowing
- **Hover a run's name** on its card, then click **History**, to see the
  [history](/docs/user-guide/history-and-dashboards) of that input.
- **Hover almost anything**: metric names show their description, values their exact number, `≠` tags explain
  reference mismatches, run names show metadata and paths to copy.
- **Full screen**: image viewers have a [full screen](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#full-screen) button (or
  <kbd>f</kbd>) to compare images on the whole screen, and each card has one at its top right.
- **Compare two batches of the same commit**: in the commit bar's **Change** menu, **Also show in the bottom bar**, then
  pick another batch in the reference bar.
- **Compare across projects**: type another project in the **Change project** box of the same menu, e.g. to compare
  two sub-projects of a monorepo.
- **Sort by a metric** in the view options to see the worst regressions first.
- **Filter with exclusions and patterns**: `-gradient`, `facade|foliage`, `denoise_strength:1.6`, `fail`,
  `status:success` (only the runs that succeeded). See the
  [filter syntax](/docs/user-guide/finding-results#filter-syntax).
- **Search the batch selector**: type `failed`, `running`, `local`, `tuning` or a user name.
- **Short hashes** work everywhere you type a commit.
- **Copy an image** with the copy button of an image viewer, and paste it straight into a chat. See
  [Copy and save](/docs/user-guide/image-viewer#copy-and-save).
