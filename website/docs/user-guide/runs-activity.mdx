Wondering whether your batch started, why it's slow, or how busy the cluster is? **Runs activity** shows the runs that
wait in a queue and run now, those that ended recently, and the batches that will start soon. Open it from your account
menu (**Runs activity**, next to **Storage usage**), from a project's sidebar, or with
[Search…](/docs/user-guide/finding-results#command-palette) (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>).

## See what runs now
The cards at the top count your runs:
- **Batches start soon**: batches you started from QA-Board whose `qa batch` waits for the cluster, e.g. a queued LSF
  job. Their runs show up once it starts. They're listed below, with their LSF job and a link to their logs.
- **Waiting**: runs `qa batch` sent to a queue (LSF, Celery…) that didn't start yet.
- **Running**: runs that started and didn't end yet. *Pending for over a day* warns about runs whose job may have died.
- **Ended** and **Failed**: the runs that ended in the time window (the last 24 hours by default).

Click **Waiting**, **Running**, **Ended** or **Failed** to list only those runs. The timeline shows how many runs
waited and ran over the window, and below it how many ended. **Busiest projects**, **queues** and, with
*Everyone's*, **users** say who and what loads the cluster: click a project or a user to see its runs, a queue to
group the runs by queue. The page refreshes every 15 seconds: switch off **Live** to pause it.

**Your runs** or **Everyone's**: by default you see your own runs. *Everyone's* shows all the runs of the projects
you can see, and who started them.

## Group runs as you like
**Group by** the batch (the default), commit, project, user, `qa batch` command, runner, queue, platform, status, or
any **job option**: type its name, e.g. `project` or `memory` for LSF. Choose **Nothing: the runs** to list the runs
themselves.

Each row counts its runs by status, says since when they're pending, and how long they wait and run on average. Click
the arrow to see its runs: their input, configuration, job options, when they were queued, started and ended, with links
to their results, their log and their files. Search filters projects, branches, batches, commits and users. Sort to
see the runs pending the longest, the latest news, or the most failures first.

Everything you choose is in the page's address: bookmark it, or share it.

## Clean up runs that are stuck
When LSF kills a job, or its machine reboots, the run can't tell QA-Board it ended: it shows as running forever.
From a row's menu (**⋯**):
- **Mark waiting and running runs as failed…** marks your runs as failed, after saying how many. Their jobs aren't
  stopped: if one still runs, it saves its results when it ends. You can
  [redo](/docs/user-guide/running-from-the-webapp#redo-stop-and-clean-up-runs) them later.
- **Stop the batch…** kills the batch's jobs and fails its runs, like **Stop runs** on the commit page.

A single run has its own **Mark as failed** button. You change only your own runs, in projects where you're a developer.
