A batch disappeared, a tuning of 2,000 runs is filling the cluster, a run's metric changed overnight: the project's
**audit log** says who did it, and when.

Open **Admin → Audit log** in the project's sidebar. Events are newest first, about the project and the subprojects you can see:
- batches and tunings started from the web app, runs redone, batches stopped, CI jobs started from the web app,
- batches, runs and milestones deleted, and old results deleted by the project's [retention](/docs/storage/deleting-old-data),
- runs marked as failed or whose metrics were edited, with their old and new values, batches renamed or moved,
- roles, visibility and access requests.

Each event says who did it, and whether with a token. **Details** shows the rest: the commit and the runner of a
batch, the parameters of a tuning, how many runs were redone.

To see the events about one batch, open its menu on the commit page and choose **Audit log of this batch**.

## Find an event
- **Category** or **Action**: e.g. *Runs and batches*, or only *Deleted a batch*.
- **By**: part of a user name, e.g. `ali` finds `alice`. Click a name in the list to see only their events.
- **About**: e.g. `batch:1234`. Click what an event is about to see only its events.
- **From** and **to**: the dates, both included.

**Export** downloads the matching events as CSV or JSON Lines.

<details>
<summary>Who can see it</summary>

The project's developers and owners, and admins. Viewers see who to ask instead. The project's audit log doesn't
show where people connected from: admins see it, with everything else, in **Admin → Audit** (see
[the audit log](/docs/backend-admin/audit-log)).

Results sent by `qa run` and CI aren't in it: each run already says who sent it.

</details>
